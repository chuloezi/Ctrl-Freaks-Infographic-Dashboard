The gap is closing, but only recently

Registration completeness, % of estimated events
The coverage gap, 2025
42.4percentage points

Births are recorded far more completely than deaths. The gap reached 61.8 points in 2022 and has narrowed every year since.

Births have levelled off. 92.9% matches the 2022 level, after dipping near 90%.

Deaths are driving the change, up from a low of 26.2% in 2021.

Speed has slipped. Deaths registered on time fell from 97.7% in 2023 to 89.9%, while births held at 99.1%.

Women's deaths are counted less. Completeness is 43.7% for women and 57.6% for men.

Completeness is not timeliness.

Completeness compares recorded events with estimated events. Timeliness asks how many recorded events met the legal deadline.

More recorded deaths is not more dying.

Registered deaths rose as coverage widened, while the adjusted death rate edged down.

A count is not a risk.

Provinces differ in population, so divorce counts are not rates, and no count here is one person's probability.
